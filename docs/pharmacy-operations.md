# Pharmacy Operations Requirements

## Licensing
- [ ] Resident pharmacy licence in NY, in good standing.
- [ ] Non-resident (out-of-state) pharmacy licence for **every** state you ship to. This is the most common gap; several states also require the pharmacist-in-charge to hold that state's licence.
- [ ] NPI, NCPDP and DEA registrations current (DEA even though no controlled substances are dispensed, if required by your state).
- [ ] 503A compounding registration and any state-specific sterile compounding permits.
- [ ] LegitScript certification, required by most ad platforms and payment processors.

## Records
- Prescription records kept per state law (commonly 5–10 years; use the longest that applies).
- Encounter notes, attestations and audit logs kept at least 6 years.
- Every dispensing record links the prescription, the pharmacist who verified it, the lot and expiry, and the shipment.

## Compounding (503A)
- Patient-specific prescriptions only; no anticipatory batching beyond what regulation allows.
- Master formulation record and batch records for each preparation.
- USP <795>/<797> standards, with beyond-use dates on every label.
- Ingredient suppliers FDA-registered, with certificates of analysis on file.
- Quality checks documented, and a recall procedure that can reach every patient who received a lot.

## Shipping
- Cold chain validated for compounded injectables: packaging, coolant, duration, and a record of the validation.
- Temperature excursion procedure, with the patient told what to do if a package arrives warm.
- Delivery confirmation retained; signature required where state law says so.

## Patient care
- Pharmacist counselling offered on every new prescription, in the way your states require.
- A pharmacist reachable during business hours, and an after-hours emergency route.
- Adverse event and medication error reporting procedure, including FDA MedWatch.
