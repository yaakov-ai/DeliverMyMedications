# Workforce Training

At hire, then yearly, and whenever policies change. Keep the sign-off sheet for six years.

## Everyone
- What PHI is, and the minimum-necessary rule.
- Looking only at records you need for your job; every view is logged.
- Passwords, authenticator apps, and never sharing accounts.
- Phishing: nobody from the pharmacy will ask for your sign-in code.
- Reporting a suspected breach within 24 hours.
- Verifying identity before discussing a record by phone.
- Never putting health details in personal email or text.

## Pharmacy staff
- Pharmacist verification steps and what each check means.
- Cold-chain handling and the 24-hour clock.
- Prescription transfer rules, and what may and may not be transferred.
- Recognizing prescription fraud and what to do.

## Providers
- The asynchronous standard of care and when to require an in-person visit or decline.
- Practising only in states where you hold a current licence.
- Documenting your reasoning in the encounter note before signing.
- Responding to patient questions within one business day.

## Admins
- Least-privilege account setup and same-day removal when someone leaves.
- Reviewing the audit log weekly.
- Verifying identity by phone before resetting anyone's two-factor.
